== Description ==

Clothing Store Lite is a modern, responsive theme specifically crafted for establishing a professional fashion shop with a clean and structured layout, making it perfect for boutiques or elegant clothing shops that offer categories like lingeries and stylish apparel. It features organized sections to showcase products, highlighted items, and promotional banners in a visually appealing manner that enhances product display. With WooCommerce integration, this theme incorporates essential ecommerce functionalities, including product pages, cart capabilities, and secure checkout, providing a comprehensive online shop experience. Additionally, it is compatible with Contact Form 7 for managing customer inquiries and Yoast SEO to boost search visibility. Offering options to customize color schemes, typography, and page layouts, it allows retailers to adapt the design to their specific needs while retaining a refined and elegant aesthetic. The lightweight and mobile-friendly structure guarantees reliable performance across desktops, tablets, and smartphones, making it an excellent choice for ecommerce websites prioritizing usability, fashion branding, and effective product presentation.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.
